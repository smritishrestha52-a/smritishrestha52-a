How to Make Ramen

What You Need

  • 1 ramen packet
  • 500ml water
  • 1 egg
  • Green onion
  • Kimchi

Steps

  1. Boil 500ml of water in a pot.
  2. Add the ramen noodles to the boiling water.
  3. Cook the noodles for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the seasoning and stir well.
  5. Add the egg and green onion.
  6. Serve the ramen while it is hot.
